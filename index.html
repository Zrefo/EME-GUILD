// ABOUT

About EME Guild

Who we are, and what we're building on HylexMC.

EME Guild is a growing HylexMC community created for players who want to be part of something more than just a guild. We aim to bring together players from different game modes, create a welcoming environment and build a strong community where everyone can find their place.

Our Goal

The goal of the EME Guild is to unite players and create a distinct community of its own on HylexMC.

// GUILD INFO

Guild Information

What it takes to join, ally with us, and how we play.

01

Joining Requirements

The requirements aren't strict — we welcome anyone who has linked their Discord account to the HylexMC server and has spent at least three days playing on the server.

02

Alliance Requirements

We require a minimum of 5 members in your guild before we'll consider an alliance.

03

Main Game Mode

We don't have a default game mode. Players from every game mode are welcome here.
