01 / THE COMMUNITY

Discord Rules

Read the Discord Community Guidelines and the Minecraft EULA — both apply here at all times.

Respect HylexMC

EME is based on HylexMC. By being part of EME, you are also part of the HylexMC community. This is not the place to insult or criticize the server.

You must also follow the HylexMC rules while here.

Advertising

Promotion of Discord or Minecraft servers other than mc.HylexMC.net is not allowed. Sharing other server names or links through messages, images, videos, or any other method is prohibited.

Chat

  • Do not mention or tag others without a valid reason.
  • No spamming or flooding the chat.
  • No swearing in public chats.
  • Be friendly, and treat others the way you want to be treated.
  • Racism, sexism, and any other kind of discrimination are never tolerated.

No politics

Political discussion of any kind is not allowed in text or voice channels. This includes parties, elections, governments, wars, activism, and politically charged topics.

No NSFW

No sexual, explicit, gore, or otherwise disturbing content. This applies to messages, images, links, usernames, nicknames, avatars, and statuses.

02 / THE GUILD

EME Guild Rules

Followed by every member, without exception.

  1. 01

    Respect every guild member.

  2. 02

    Do not kill other guild members.

  3. 03

    Do not betray guild members.

  4. 04

    Do not intentionally cause problems within the guild.

  5. 05

    Represent EME positively.

  6. 06

    Help other guild members when possible.

  7. 07

    Support guild members during conflicts.

  8. 08

    Share important information with the guild.

  9. 09

    Participate in guild activities.

  10. 10

    Do not leave a guild member behind when they need help.

  11. 11

    Do not share private information accessible only to members of our guild.

  12. 12

    Follow reasonable decisions made by guild leadership.

  13. 13

    Follow the HylexMC server rules.
