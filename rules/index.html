Guild Rules

The few rules every member is expected to follow.

01

Guild Conduct

Respect every guild member.
Do not kill other guild members.
Do not betray guild members.
Do not intentionally cause problems within the guild.
Represent EME positively.

02

Teamwork

Help other guild members when possible.
Support guild members during conflicts.
Share important information with the guild.
Participate in guild activities.
Do not leave a guild member behind when they need help.

03

Other

Do not share private information accessible only to members of our guild.
Follow reasonable decisions made by guild leadership.
Follow the HylexMC server rules!

Read the full HylexMC server rules for details that apply server-wide.
