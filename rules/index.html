EME RULES

DISCORD RULES

Always follow the Discord Community Guidelines and the Minecraft EULA.

Read the Discord Community Guidelines and the Minecraft EULA — both apply here at all times.

RESPECT HYLEXMC

HylexMC is a Minecraft server where the EME guild is placed. This means that by being part of the EME guild community, you also belong to the HylexMC community.

This is not the place to insult or criticize the HylexMC server.

You are also required to follow HylexMC's rules while here — read them here.

ADVERTISING

We do not allow the promotion of any Discord or Minecraft servers other than mc.HylexMC.net.

Sharing the names or links of other servers via messages, images, videos, or any other method is prohibited.

CHAT

  • Do not mention or tag others without a valid reason.
  • No spamming or flooding the chat.
  • No swearing in public chats.
  • Be friendly, and treat others the way you want to be treated.
  • Racism, sexism and any other kind of discrimination are never tolerated.

NO POLITICS

Political discussion of any kind is not allowed. This includes parties, elections, governments, wars, activism and any other politically charged topic, in text or in voice.

NO NSFW

No sexual, explicit, gore or otherwise disturbing content. This covers messages, images, links, usernames, nicknames, avatars and statuses.
