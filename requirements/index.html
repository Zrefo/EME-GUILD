01 / BECOME A MEMBER

Join the guild

Ready to become part of EME? Make sure you meet both requirements.

  • 01

    Link your Discord account

    Your Discord account must be linked to your HylexMC account.

  • 02

    At least 3 days of playtime

    You must have at least 3 days (72 hours) of total in-game playtime on HylexMC.

    Your guild must have a minimum of 5 members.
